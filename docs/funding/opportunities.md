# Funding opportunities

The catalogue below lists every programme that plausibly fits University Assistant, grouped by the four routes from the [overview](README.md). Each entry state the funder, the money, who may apply, the next deadline as of 19 October 2026, a fit rating for *this* project, and the source. The fit ratings were revised after the checks in [verified-findings.md](verified-findings.md); where the two disagree, the verified findings win.

**Fit ratings**

- **Strong** — the subject, the applicant type and the timing all line up, or would with one concrete change.
- **Partial** — the subject fits but the applicant type, scope or stage does not, without a partner or a legal step.
- **Weak** — possible in principle, but the project is the wrong shape or stage for it.

Amounts and dates are the funder's published figures at the time of writing. Confirm at the source before relying on them.

## Route A — Open-source and public-interest tech

These fund the software prototype itself. They are the only grants open to an individual with no company and no university partner.

## Route B — University spin-off and startup

These fund the preparation of a company, not just a prototype. They are the route to turn the demo into a service, and they are the reason to talk to the university's transfer office early. University of Stuttgart's entry point is **TRACES (Transfer Center)** and **TTI GmbH**, which can host a team as a "technology and start-up enterprise" (TGU) before a company exists.

### EXIST-Gründerstipendium

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Funder** | Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action (BMWK); administered by Projektträger Jülich (PtJ) |
| **Money** | Living stipend of **€1,000/mo** (students), €2,000 (skilled professionals), €2,500 (graduates), €3,000 (doctorates), plus €150/child; up to **€30,000** materials and **€5,000** coaching. Up to 12 months, up to three people |
| **Who** | Students who have completed **at least half** of their studies, graduates up to five years out, and researchers — **applying through their university's start-up network**, with a professor as mentor. Teams must not be majority students |

## Route C — Scholarships for the person

These fund the student, not the app. They are the fastest money, and they are the right answer if the goal is simply to keep building without a company.

### Deutschlandstipendium (University of Stuttgart)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Money** | **€300/month** (half from a private sponsor, half from the federal government), income-independent, at least two semesters |
| **Who** | Enrolled students of all nationalities with good grades **and** at least one additional criterion — engagement, special circumstances, awards, an unusual biography. Awarded directly by the university |
| **Deadline** | The Uni Stuttgart round for 2026/27 is closed; the round for **2027/28 is expected to be published around December 2026** |
| **Fit** | **Strong for time-to-build.** Building a public-interest study tool is a textbook "additional criterion" (social/ecological engagement, special qualifications). It does not fund the project, but it funds the months |

## Route D — Higher-education teaching innovation

These fund improvements to teaching and learning. They fit the *content* best, but they require a lecturer or the university as the applicant.

### Stiftung Innovation in der Hochschullehre (StIL) — "Freiraum" and "Fokus Netzwerke"

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Funder** | Stiftung Innovation in der Hochschullehre (a trust under the Toepfer Stiftung), funded by the federal and state governments |
| **Money** | Freiraum 2026: ~**€46M** across 143 projects. Fokus Netzwerke 2026: 29 projects. Laufzeit 12–24 months |
| **Who** | Teachers (Lehrende) at German higher-education institutions, or consortia of institutions. There is no thematic/faculty restriction for Freiraum; an **expression of interest plus a lottery** precedes the substantive selection |
| **Deadline** | Freiraum 2026 started 1 April 2026; the **next Freiraum round begins at the start of 2027**. Fokus Netzwerke runs its own cycles |
| **Fit** | **Strong content, wrong applicant.** A study assistant that reduces the friction of C@MPUS, ILIAS and module planning is exactly a "Studium und Lehre" innovation. But a solo student cannot apply; this needs a professor to lead. Approach a lecturer with the demo as the proof |
| **Source** | [stiftung-hochschullehre.de/foerderung/freiraum](https://stiftung-hochschullehre.de/foerderung/freiraum), [stiftung-hochschullehre.de/foerderung](https://stiftung-hochschullehre.de/foerderung), [e-teaching.org](https://www.e-teaching.org/news/termine/ausschreibung-der-freiraum-foerderung-2026) |
