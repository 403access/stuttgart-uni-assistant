## How to keep this current

Grant deadlines and budgets move. When you update this section:

1. Re-check the funder's own page, not a summary, for the deadline and the amount.
2. Update both the [opportunities catalogue](opportunities.md) and the [calendar](application-playbook.md).
3. Record the date you re-checked in the table header here.
