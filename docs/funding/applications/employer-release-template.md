# Employer release confirmation (Freistellungsbestätigung)

The Prototype Fund funds solo developers who are **self-employed/freelance** *or* **released part-time by their employer for the project period**. If you are employed, this letter is the cheapest way to meet that rule. The programme states the exception explicitly: "oder für die Projektlaufzeit (anteilig) vom Arbeitgeber freigestellt".

Ask the employer — the company owned by your mother — to issue it on company letterhead, signed by an authorised signatory. The funded period is **1 June 2027 – 30 November 2027** for the six-month phase, or **1 June 2027 – 31 March 2028** for the ten-month Resilienz track.

> This is a template, not legal or tax advice. Adapt it to the actual employment relationship. If you reduce your hours, clarify pay, social insurance and vacation with the employer and, if needed, a Steuerberater.

## German template

```text
[Firmenbriefkopf]

Freistellungsbestätigung

Hiermit bestätigt die [Firma], dass [Vor- und Nachname], geboren am [Geburtsdatum],
wohnhaft [Anschrift], bei uns als [Position] beschäftigt ist.

Für die Teilnahme am Förderprogramm „Prototype Fund“ (Förderlinie Resilienz,
Förderzeitraum 01.06.2027 bis 31.03.2028) stellen wir [Vor- und Nachname]
im Zeitraum vom 01.06.2027 bis 31.03.2028 anteilig in folgendem Umfang frei:

    [z. B. 50 % der vertraglichen Arbeitszeit / 20 Stunden pro Woche]

Das Arbeitsverhältnis wird in diesem Zeitraum im Übrigen fortgeführt.
Die Freistellung erfolgt ausschließlich zum Zweck der Arbeit am oben genannten
Förderprojekt. Eine Doppelfinanzierung derselben Arbeitszeit durch das
Arbeitsverhältnis und die Förderung ist ausgeschlossen.

[Ort], [Datum]

[Unterschrift, Name, Funktion]
[Stempel der Firma]
```

## English translation

```text
[Company letterhead]

Confirmation of partial release

[Company] confirms that [full name], born [date of birth], residing at [address],
is employed by us as [position].

For participation in the "Prototype Fund" programme (Resilienz line, funding
period 1 June 2027 to 31 March 2028), we release [full name] on a part-time
basis from 1 June 2027 to 31 March 2028 as follows:

    [e.g. 50% of contractual working time / 20 hours per week]

The employment relationship continues in the remaining scope during this period.
The release is granted solely for the purpose of working on the funded project.
Double funding of the same working time through the employment relationship and
the grant is excluded.

[Place], [date]

[Signature, name, role]
[Company stamp]
```

## What to check before you use it

- **The signatory is authorised** to represent the company.
- **The period matches the application** (six or ten months).
- **The percentage is realistic.** The grant is meant to fund the project hours; the release should cover the time you will actually spend on it.
- **No double dip.** The same hours must not be paid twice — once by the employer and once by the grant.
- **Keep the original** for the formal application stage in March 2027; the first-stage form in November 2026 only needs the fact that you qualify.

## If the employer will not release you

Then the alternatives are:

1. **Register as a freelancer** (*Freiberufler*) with the Finanzamt. Check the employment contract for consent to secondary self-employment, and keep the two activities distinct.
2. **Apply as a GbR team** seated in Germany (up to four people). The GbR is the applicant; team members may be employed.

Do not submit a solo application while none of these is in place; the formal check would reject it.
