# Studying: modules, planning, and peers

The **Studium (Study)** tab is the academic record: your program, modules with grades and locks, the requirements tracker, the suggested learning sequence, and the people around it. Except where noted, this reads the bundled demonstration record.

## The tab

| Screen | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Studium | Program overview: degree, total ECTS, your module list with state (passed, enrolled, open) and grades |
| Module detail (tap a module) | Topics, ECTS, prerequisites, Tutorialgruppe, exam form, lecturer, literature, past exams link |
| Anforderungen | The requirements tracker: area meters against the plan, what is still open, and how far you are versus the pace you would need for 30 ECTS per semester |
| Reihenfolge | The suggested learning sequence: which modules are locked until prerequisites pass, and a next-semester suggestion of at most 30 ECTS |

Working with the record:

- **Marking a module as planned** sets a local note ("I intend to take this"). It does not change earned credits.
- **Anrechnung (recognition)** is a draft on the device: where a course came from (abroad or a previous study), institution, course name, ECTS, grade, and the target module to recognize it into, plus a checklist of the papers to collect. Nothing is submitted anywhere. A passed target is flagged.

## Lecturers and tutorials

| Screen | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Dozenten / Dozent profile | Institute, consultation hours, demo e-mail, research area, and office room |
| Tutorien | The exercise groups of a module: group, room, seats, and whether the demonstration student is enrolled |

## Peers (Kommilitonen)

Ratings and comments from fellow students, on this record seeded and yours added on top.

| What you do | Where | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read reviews | `/kommilitonen` or a module detail | Rating, hours per week, and difficulty for the big modules |
| Write a review | A module's review section | Stored on this device only, shown next to the seeded reviews |
| Share a note | Documents, or the note's own screen | Marked shared; peers on **this device** can see it in Kommilitonen. There is no real transfer between phones in this build. |
| Past exams | Altklausuren | Topics, focus areas, and a study hint per exam. There is no exam PDF in this build. |
